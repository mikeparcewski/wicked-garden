# Spoken voice: Say lines and speaker notes

A demo script's **Say** lines and a deck's **speaker notes** are what a presenter says out loud. Write them the way
a person talks while showing a colleague something, not the way a product describes itself.

## How a person talks

- Talk to the audience ("you", "we", "I"), or about the person in the story by name. Use contractions
  ("it's", "you'll", "we've").
- Say why before what: the reason or the problem first, then the click or the number.
- Connect the beats: "so", "now", "here's the thing", "and that's why it matters".
- Vary sentence length. A short one lands. A longer one carries the explanation that goes with it.
- Talk about what the audience can do or notice, not what the system "reads", "processes" or "reconciles". When the
  product has to be the subject, keep the verb plain ("it checks each row against the ledger").
- Don't read the screen or the slide back. Say what it means, or what to look at.

## Patterns to avoid

| Pattern | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| "X, not Y" slogan | "The day starts ranked, not read." | "Here's what needs you first this morning." |
| Colon-takeaway headline | "Build report: I press **Build report** to create the month-end report." | "Let's do the month-end report now. I'll press **Build report** and we'll watch it run." |
| Noun stack or fragment | "Month-end, one click." | "Now let's build the month-end report." |
| System as subject | "The job reads the ledger, reconciles it and drafts the summary." | "It has to match the ledger, so the draft you get is already checked against it." |
| Filler ("seamlessly", "leverage", "robust", "tailored", "effortlessly") | "Suggested questions are tailored to the record." | "You don't start from a blank box. These questions are about this request." |

## Speaker notes

Notes are the talk track for that slide, in the presenter's own words. Not a stage direction, not process notes, not
a second copy of the slide, and no fact the slide's sources don't support.

| Slide | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Timeline: 23% → 61% in the first run, 72% → 79% in the second; the count's definition changed in between | "Point at the definition change between the first two rows." | "See the jump from 61% to 72% between the first two rows? That's where we changed what we count, so those two numbers don't compare directly." |
| What next: close the open placeholders, domain by domain | "Next, we close the open placeholders, domain by domain." | "So what's left? The open placeholders. We'll close them one domain at a time." |

## Read-aloud check

Say every line out loud at talking speed. If you'd stumble, run out of breath, or never say it to someone sitting
next to you, rewrite it.
