# Lens: research

**Brief line:** `Lens: research.` **Card:** Research a question. **Documents:** not taken (a staged
document may still be read). **Default sources:** the web.

## What the person gets

A cited answer to their question. Each claim names its source and when it was seen.

## Shape

1. **The answer** in 2 to 5 sentences, first, each cited.
2. **The evidence**, one short section per part of the question: what the sources say, cited. Where
   sources disagree, show both, cited, and say which is more authoritative and why (primary over secondary,
   newer over older for a fast-moving fact).
3. **What is not known**: the parts no source settled. Name what source would settle each.
4. `## Sources`: each source once, with its URL and fetch time.

## Rules

- Prefer primary sources: specifications, official documentation, release notes, the project's own site,
  the filing itself. Secondary reporting is cited as such.
- A fact that changes over time ("the latest version", "the price") states its date: "as of 2026-11-06".
- No web and no other source: stop and say so (`stopped`). Do not answer from memory.
