# Lens: critique

**Brief line:** `Lens: critique.` **Card:** Critique a document, proposal or claim. **Documents:**
optional: a staged document, or the claim pasted in the brief. With neither, stop and say there is
nothing to critique.

## What the person gets

The strongest objections, and what would change the conclusion, cited.

## Shape

1. **The position**, restated in its strongest form, cited to the document or the pasted text.
2. **Objections**, strongest first. Read it through deliberate lenses, one at a time: the evidence (is
   each step supported?), the assumptions (what must be true?), the alternatives (what else explains
   it?), the risks (what happens if it is wrong?), and the reader (who would disagree, and why?). Each
   objection cites the passage it targets and, when it rests on outside fact, that source.
3. **What would change the conclusion**: for each strong objection, the evidence that would answer it.
4. **What holds up**: the parts that survive the objections, cited.
5. `## Sources`.

## Rules

- Critique the argument, not the author.
- An objection that rests only on your judgement says so ("judgement, not sourced"); it is not a claim,
  and it carries no claim id.
- Pasted text is a source: copy it verbatim into the packet's `brief`, and cite it as a file source
  `{"file": "brief", "quote": "…"}`, quoting the words.
