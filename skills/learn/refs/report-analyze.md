# Lens: analyze

**Brief line:** `Lens: analyze.` **Card:** Analyze documents. **Documents:** required. With none staged,
`gather` refuses (the preflight exits 3) and says the person must attach them.

## What the person gets

The structure, the key claims, the numbers and the contradictions of the documents, cited.

## Shape

1. **What these are**: each document's kind, author or owner when stated, date, and length.
2. **Structure**: the sections and how they relate, in a short outline.
3. **Key claims**: what the documents assert, each cited with its quote.
4. **Numbers**: every material figure in a table (value, unit, what it measures, where), cited. Recompute
   totals and percentages, and flag any that do not add up.
5. **Contradictions and gaps**: where documents disagree with each other or themselves, both sides cited.
6. The intent's own question, when the brief asks one, answered from the above.
7. `## Sources`: each file, with the pages cited.

## Rules

- Report what the documents say, not whether it is true, unless the brief asks; then cite the outside
  source that tests it.
- A pdf with no extractable text can still be quoted from the original; label such a claim "from a pdf
  without text".
