# Lens: due-diligence

**Brief line:** `Lens: due-diligence.` **Card:** Due diligence. **Documents:** required. With none staged,
`gather` refuses (the preflight exits 3) and says the person must attach them.

## What the person gets

What holds, what is missing, and what is a risk, each cited to file and page, or to a quote.

## Shape

1. **Scope**: the documents read (all of them, by name) and the question the brief asks.
2. **What holds**: the commitments and facts the documents establish, cited.
3. **What is missing**: what a document of this kind normally carries and these do not (acceptance
   criteria, liability, termination, data handling, service levels, a named owner). Cite the section
   where it should be, or say no section covers it. An absence is a claim too: cite what you searched.
4. **Risks**: a matrix of likelihood by impact (low, medium, high), one row per risk, each with its
   evidence cited and what would reduce it.
5. **Contradictions** between documents, both sides cited.
6. `## Sources`: each file, with the pages cited.

## Rules

- Quote; do not paraphrase a commitment. The quote is the source record.
- A pdf with no extractable text can still be quoted from the original; label such a claim "from a pdf
  without text".
- The web, when picked, is context only (a regulation, a company's filing), cited as a web source.
